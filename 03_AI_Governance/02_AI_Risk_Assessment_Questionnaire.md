## 8. RISK EVALUATION AND CLASSIFICATION



The assessor must evaluate the responses provided in Sections 2â€“7 and record supporting evidence.



### 8.1 Assessment Rating



| Response | Evaluation |

|---|---|

| Yes | Control is implemented and supporting evidence is available. |

| No | Control is missing, ineffective or not adequately demonstrated. |

| N/A | Control is not applicable; justification must be documented. |



A Yes response must not be accepted without appropriate supporting evidence where the control requires verification.



### 8.2 Risk Classification



| Risk Level | Description | Required Action |

|---|---|---|

| Low | Controls are implemented and no significant unresolved risks are identified. | Routine monitoring and approval. |

| Medium | Some control weaknesses exist but can be addressed through documented remediation. | Conditional approval and remediation tracking. |

| High | Significant privacy, security, legal or operational weaknesses exist. | Escalation to management and remediation before deployment. |

| Critical | Severe or unacceptable risks exist, including unauthorised sensitive-data exposure or uncontrolled AI actions. | Do not approve deployment until the risks are resolved and reassessed. |



The assessor must document the reasons for the assigned risk level. A numerical score must not override a critical privacy or security finding.



## 9. REQUIRED EVIDENCE



The following evidence should be collected where applicable:



\- AI system inventory and business approval.

\- Data-flow diagrams and data classification records.

\- Privacy assessments and lawful-basis documentation.

\- Data Processing Agreements and vendor security assessments.

\- Training-data provenance and licensing documentation.

\- Prompt-injection and security testing results.

\- Human oversight procedures.

\- AI interaction and output logging configuration.

\- Incident response and business continuity procedures.

\- Remediation plans for identified weaknesses.
